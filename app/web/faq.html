NooK FAQ

Quick answers to common questions about installation, troubleshooting, and using NooK encrypted SMS messenger. This page will be translated by Google Translate just like the main page. If your question is not answered here, contact me please at support@semweb.solutions

My communication partner and I setup NooK to try it. With my partner I cannot exchange (encrypt/decrypt) messages. Each of us receives the coded SMS but NooK does not show the decrypted message.

Control the version of each NooK APP on the relative smartphones. The version is shown in NooK at its very bottom with a green text on dark grey background. If the versions do not match, reinstall NooK using the button "Upgrade NooK" from the settings or install a current NooK version from NooK's website. The higher the version, the better you go.

I just defined a new chat picking the number from the telephone list. The name appears with weird characters. What should I do?

This was an old bug which should have been fixed now. Just delete and recreate that chat. The name should appear properly.

I just wrote a message inside an encrypted chat but NooK shows me weird characters instead of the message plaintext I typed.

This should be fixed in the current NooK version. Just reinstall NooK using a current version.

I lost my smartphone. So I setup a new NooK and setup in a chat with my partner the encryption parameters we shared. Unfortunately I cannot communicate any more

The encryption parameters must be exactly the same in order to be able to communicate in NooK. On the whole you have to correctly set 4 parameters: Encoding (base32, base64 or base256), Encoding password (at least 8 characters), Encryption method (currently there is only SiSa - AES256) and Encryption password. Possibly you made a small mistake (a single char is enough to break en/de-cryption) - best practice here: speak at the telephone these settings. Do not use plaintext e-mailing or plaintext-SMS! What you can do in those cases where you cannot easily speak at the phone with your partner, is to use the Default encryption setting (available from NooK 1.6.8 on). Press the gear in the chat bar, press DEFAULT and save. The chat encryption parameters are reset. You use only base256 with no password (not the strengthst protecion) but for a single message exchange this will to. Share your encryption data, set and save them and get back to communicate in real privacy.

I received an orange message saying that decryption could not happen succesfully

You and your parnter do not have the same NooK APPs or you or your partner changed (per mistake) the encryption settings. This caused that the encryption parameters used by yourself and your partner are not the same and therefore NooK on your phone cannot decrypt what Nook on your partner's phone encrypted. Check together the encryption settings and adapt them.

I tried to install NooK on Android 15 but it does not allow it to use SMS

Unfortunately, there are some Android versions which handles APPs accessing SMS as dangerous. NooK was tested on Android 13, Android 14 and Android 16. There is currently also an issue on Android 16 on Lineage 23 - same issue. Please install another OS to use Nook (sorry...)!
