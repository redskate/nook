Welcome to NooK! (No one Knows)

Private SMS Messaging Powered by AES-256 Encryption. Secure. Simple. Offline.

NOOK transforms your everyday SMS into truly private conversations. Built directly on the cellular network, it requires zero internet connection and collects absolutely no data. Your privacy isn't just a feature—it's the foundation.

✨ Why Choose NooK?

• Military-Grade Encryption: Your messages are secured with AES-256 before they ever leave your device.
• No Internet? No Problem: Communicate securely using the existing SMS infrastructure, even in areas with no data signal.
• Your Data Stays Yours: We don't collect, store, or share anything. No trackers, no analytics, no cloud servers.
• Open Source & Transparent: The entire codebase is public for anyone to audit. No secrets, no backdoors.

⚠️ Note: The app requires SMS permissions to function. This triggers Google Play Protect warnings, but the app is 100% safe and open source. No internet access means your data cannot be leaked.

🔒 First-Time User Tips:
• Set up App Lock (fingerprint/PIN) immediately
• Share passwords with contacts through a secure channel (voice/in person)
• Only message content is encrypted - metadata (phone numbers, timestamps) remains visible to carriers
